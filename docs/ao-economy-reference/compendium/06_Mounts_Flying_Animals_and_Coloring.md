# Ridable Travel Animals & Coloring Loop
Aerial companions tie children's coloring, the coin economy, non-verbal emote language, and portal transit together.

## Mount Roster & Speed Requirements
1. **Rainbow Unicorn:** Speed Mount (Speed / Cost: 500 Coins)
2. **Armored Light Pegasus:** Scout Mount (Speed / Cost: 650 Coins)
3. **Armored Dark Pegasus:** Tank Mount (Speed / Cost: 800 Coins)
4. **White Unicorn:** Healer Mount (Speed / Cost: 950 Coins)
5. **Cosmic Dragon:** Legendary Flight Mount (Speed / Cost: 1200 Coins)

## Physical-to-Digital Coloring Loop
In Anom's Corner, children color printable line-art pages ("Paint Your Ridables!") and upload them to earn rewards (+5 coins per finished page, +25 for contest entry), which unlocks the exact ridable mount in the game explorer.
