# AO ALIVE Living Design Standard
## The Principle
> "Alive is not a color. It's the timing."

## Staggered Motion Loops
Elements pulse organically on non-synchronized periods so they drift in and out of phase:
- **House 1 (Pixel & Dot / Moonberry Farm):** 3.2s period
- **House 2 (Clifford & Tater Security):** 3.6s period
- **House 3 (Mood Buddies):** 3.9s period
- **House 4 (Sassy Patrol Guardians):** 4.1s period
- **AO Logo Pulse:** 2.6s period
- **Orbit Periods:** 3.8s (inner), 4s (moon), 6s (outer mount), 7s (hub mount)

## Palette Anchors
- **Electric Cyan:** `#00eaff` (Primary accent, Pixel, Explorer signatures)
- **Hot Magenta:** `#ff00c8` (Secondary accent, Dot, energy)
- **Celestial Gold:** `#d8ae55` (Prestige, borders, cards, rules)
- **Void Ground:** `#04040a` (Homeworld/marketing) / `#0A0A10` (App shell) / `#000000` (Full-bleed canvas)

## Three-Color Pulse Ring Signature
Hero elements feature a 3-tier stacked glow ring:
- **Inner:** 18px Electric Cyan (`rgba(0,234,255,0.6)`)
- **Mid:** 36px Hot Magenta (`rgba(255,0,200,0.35)`)
- **Outer:** 54px Celestial Gold (`rgba(216,174,85,0.2)`)

## Typography
- **Display/Headlines:** Space Mono
- **Body/UI:** Inter
- **Technical/HUD:** JetBrains Mono (with 0.2em-0.3em letter-spacing)
