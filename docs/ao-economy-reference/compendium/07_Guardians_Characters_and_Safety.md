## Five Age Tiers ("Independent feel, monitored safety")
1. **Sprouts (Ages 5-8):** Color & Watch. Preset avatars, reactions only, 100% Guardian-approved.
2. **Explorers (Ages 9-12):** Learn & Help. Prompts, public lounge, mod queue transitioning to AI filters. Must pass emote recognition & S.T.O.P. method.
3. **Builders (Ages 13-15):** Create & Teach. Real-time AI filtering, whitelisted links. Must pass Link Detective & Debugging Day.
4. **Architects (Ages 16-17):** Lead & Showcase. Guardians-in-training, paid commissions with parent co-sign.
5. **Guardians (Ages 18+):** Protect & Foster. Full moderation suite and governance.

## Safety Innovations
- **Optimistic-UI Moderation:** Client sees posts instantly while background checks route to Guardian Queue (<2h approval).
- **Confidence, Not Surveillance:** Parents see growth meters, not keystroke logs.
