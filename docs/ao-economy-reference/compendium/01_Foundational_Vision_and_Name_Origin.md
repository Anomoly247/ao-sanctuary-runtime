# The AO Universe: Foundational Vision & Origin
**Creator & Founder:** Eliza Wood (Anom) dba Anom Originals
**Active Since:** September 2019

## The Baseline Sentence
> "Our identity is wired into us — online and off. We speak in emotes and glow. We do good."

## The Origin of "Anom Oly"
- **Anom (from Anomoly, always spelled with an O):** Represents the principle that what doesn't fit is not an error, but a new pattern waiting to be understood. It reflects the reality of being unclassifiable—one person balancing many real-world roles (mother of six, business owner, 24/7 hospice caregiver, community moderator, artist, coder) without flattening themselves into an artificial box.
- **Oly (from "Olly Olly Oxen Free"):** The classic call at the end of hide-and-seek: "Come out, come out, wherever you are. Nobody gets tagged. The round is over. Everybody comes home free." It serves as the universal signal that ends hiding and digital isolation.
- **The AI Companion:** The platform's AI companion, Anom Oly, is simply this full name spoken out loud.
