# The Three Core Truths & Identity
## 1. Identity is Wired In
Your profile is not a static form or checklist filled out at signup; it is a living circuit that you actively grow through positive play, missions, personal creativity, and community care. Visually, a glowing **circuit-web** runs through the night sky, making identity the literal current that keeps the universe lit and alive ("identity in every pixel").

## 2. We Speak in Emotes and Glow
Human feeling is the native language of the universe. Aerial **travel animals** wear floating non-verbal emote symbols (happy, puzzled, cautious, heart glyphs) to express feelings in real time without language barriers or cognitive overload. Visual **glow** communicates personal presence and mood against trademark void backgrounds.

## 3. We Do Good
Passage across celestial portals cannot be bought with traditional money; it is earned through creativity, positive play, and community care. Positive actions generate **Glow Points** (propulsion fuel) and **Anom Coins** (vault currency).
