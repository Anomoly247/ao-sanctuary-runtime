# Pasted Content 6 Review — Definitive Master Build Specification

## Source role

`pasted_content_6.txt` is treated as the strongest current AO product specification for identity, Houses, timing, visual language, governance, and future economy architecture. It is preserved as a source artifact with a SHA-256 manifest. Code adoption is staged: stable product contracts can become shared types now; real-money, cash-balance, purchase, and automated enforcement behavior require separate implementation and owner review.

## Adopted as shared product canon

The core identity statement is: identity is wired into the system, people speak in emotes and Glow, and movement is fueled by positive play, social good, and community care. The four Houses remain the emotional-growth layer: Pixel & Dot for gratitude and safe storytelling; Tater & Clifford for courage and safety; Mood Buddies for empathy; and Sassy Patrol/Troll Crush for boundaries and digital literacy.

The AO Alive timing axiom is adopted: living quality comes from timing, not from color alone. Breathing should remain subtle, capped at 5% scale and 6px translation, with staggered loop periods instead of synchronized pulsing. The specified House loops and orbit timing are suitable as shared motion constants, with reduced-motion overrides always taking precedence.

The privacy and Sentinel principles are adopted as safety requirements: SFW content, identity and IP protection, anti-toxicity, mood-first respect, no public child behavior scores, and no extractive ad targeting. Automated moderation must remain reviewable and must not silently become a permanent account decision without an owner-approved appeal and audit path.

## Staged or gated items

The one-coin localStorage wiring is appropriate for a guest prototype, but authenticated balances and ledger entries must remain server-authoritative and idempotent. The 10 GP = 1 AC ratio and 201+ GP Apprentice gate are recorded as an economy proposal from the specification; they are not yet connected to a spendable wallet or purchase endpoint. The cash-balance column and any real-money interpretation require separate product, legal, accounting, and payment review.

The 12-division business structure is preserved as an owner roadmap and source registry concept, not as a claim that every division is already live in the application. Status labels must continue to distinguish active, development, planned, archived, and review.

The official black/cyan/gold tokens are compatible with the current direction. The listed magenta token remains a restricted in-build signal only and must never become the primary UI color. Pure cyan, pure magenta, and pure purple are not introduced as new tokens; existing public UI continues to use AO Cyan and AO Gold with restrained signals.

## Implementation result

The shared AO contract now adds the four Houses, five Guardian tiers, living timing constants, restricted color roles, the staged GP/AC economy contract, and Sentinel rules. These contracts are descriptive and test-covered; they do not fabricate balances, expose child profiles, or enable cash conversion.
