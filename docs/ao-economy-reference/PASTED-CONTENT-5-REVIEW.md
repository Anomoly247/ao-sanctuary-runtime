# Pasted Content 5 Review — AO SEL Architecture

## Source role

`pasted_content_5.txt` is preserved as a supporting educational and product-architecture reference. It is not executable product code and does not override the current AO world contract, privacy rules, or economy decisions.

## Canon reinforced

The strongest contribution is the non-extractive architecture: care should generate Glow without public child profiles, behavior scores, ad targeting, like-count competition, or intrusive collection. This aligns with the current Sanctuary direction and with Moonberry Farm’s private Growth Note design.

The Four-Step Method—Notice, Make room, Create, Connect—is a useful interaction frame for calm onboarding, reflection, creative contribution, and social handoffs. It can guide future copy and activity design without becoming a performance score.

The four SEL Houses are consistent with the previously archived Master Anom Hub reference: Pixel and Dot represent gratitude and safe storytelling; Tater and Clifford represent courage and safety; Mood Buddies represent empathy and emotional transformation; and Sassy Patrol/Troll Crush represent boundaries and digital literacy.

The Guardian Tier Tree and Mentor Loop are also consistent: Helper, Kind, Brave, Guardian, and Ultra. These should be modeled as private capability/progression states with guardian controls, not as public rankings.
