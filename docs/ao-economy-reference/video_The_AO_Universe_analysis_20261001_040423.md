Here is a summary of the AO Universe ecosystem as presented in the video, organized by the requested sections:

**Universe Premise**
The AO Universe is pitched as a "fully realized celestial town in space" designed as a permanent sanctuary rather than a standard, attention-harvesting social network. Rooted in the philosophy of founder Eliza Wood—who spent 19 years running a cleaning company and providing hospice care—the universe is built on human empathy. The foundational motto is, "We protect the glow, not the grind." The core premise is that identity is an aspiration you build towards, and moderation is treated as a form of care, not punishment. 

**Worlds and Portals**
The ecosystem is structured as a "constellation" rather than a scrolling grid or catalog. It consists of 12 distinct "live destinations" (including the Sanctuary home base, an Arcade, and a Kids Corner) that spatially relate to one another. Instead of clicking standard hyperlinks to navigate, users travel between these locations via celestial portals using cosmic mounts. 

**Contribution Economy**
The ecosystem operates on an "Earned, Not Bought" philosophy, creating a non-extractive loop. Users engage in purposeful play or community care, which earns them rewards. These rewards are then used as literal fuel to travel the universe or as resources to build out personal digital spaces. The system is designed to require active participation rather than allowing users to simply buy their way to the top.

**Anom Coins versus Glow Points**
The economy utilizes a dual-reward system:
*   **Glow Points:** These are entirely non-monetary and are earned through positive social actions, good reception, and caring for the community.
*   **Anom Coins:** These act as a premium utility token. They are used specifically to build out digital worlds and acquire rare travel mounts. 

**Mounts**
Users navigate the universe riding "cosmic mounts," depicted visually as glowing cyber-dragons. These mounts are deeply tied to the user's identity and emotional state. The design of the mounts incorporates the user's energy signatures, and they feature floating "emote crowns" above their heads that update in real-time to visually express the traveler's internal feelings as they fly.

**Social-good Missions**
The platform incentivizes social good by directly tying it to the economy. Earning Glow Points requires engaging in "community care" and receiving positive feedback from others, reinforcing the founder's ethos of dignity and "family over followers."

**Age-aware Safety**
While not exhaustively detailed, the platform's commitment to safety is indicated by its core principle of "moderation as care, not punishment" and the inclusion of a dedicated "Kids Corner" among its 12 main live destinations, reflecting a space built for varying age groups by a founder who raised six children.

**Concrete Numbers or Rules vs. Visual Mood**
*   **Stated Rules & Numbers:** 
    *   There are exactly **12** live divisions.
    *   The "AO ALIVE Standard" dictates UI animation: elements must have staggered animation periods (e.g., **3.2 seconds** for one element, **3.6 seconds** for another) so they drift in and out of sync, simulating breathing rather than a static pulse.
    *   Strict visual contrast rules are enforced using a specific palette: Void (**#0A0A10**), AO Cyan (**#00eaff**), AO Magenta (**#ff00c8**), and AO Gold (**#d8ae55**).
*   **Visual Mood:** The aesthetic result of these rules is a vibrant, "cyberpunk cosmos" featuring bright neon, glassmorphic elements, and glowing circuit patterns set against deep, dark backgrounds.
