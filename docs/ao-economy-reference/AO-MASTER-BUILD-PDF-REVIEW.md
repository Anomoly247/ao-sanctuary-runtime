# AO Universe Master Build PDF Review

## Source and authority

The attached `ao_universe_master_build_specification.pdf` is a five-page, confidential master-build specification and is archived with a SHA-256 manifest. It substantially confirms pasted_content_6 and should be treated as the higher-fidelity source for stable design contracts, while preserving explicit gates around payment, identity, moderation, and deployment.

## Stable rules adopted

The PDF confirms the core directives: identity is wired into the system; users speak in emotes and Glow; the platform protects the Glow rather than the grind; and “Alive is not a color. It’s the timing.” It confirms the AO Cyan, AO Gold, Void, True Black, Panel, and Deep Recess surface hierarchy; the prohibition on pure web-neons, withdrawn purple/navy colors, and office-document typography; and the staggered House and mount-orbit timing table.

It also confirms the 12-division operating map, the 10 GP = 1 AC proposal, the 201+ GP Apprentice threshold, the normalized wallet/ledger direction, distinct mount profiles, and the Sentinel/community-ambassador concepts. The stable subset is represented in shared AO contracts and provenance records.

## Sensitive or gated items

| Item | Current treatment |
|---|---|
| Cash balance, payment infrastructure, revenue routing, exchange-rate language | Reference architecture only. No real-money conversion, purchase, or cash ledger is enabled. |
| Wallet and ledger SQL schemas | Future server-contract direction. Must be designed against the existing database and migration workflow; no schema is copied blindly from the PDF. |
| Identity Passport requiring unobstructed face, handwritten sign, or full identity display | Not adopted. It conflicts with privacy-first child safety and would require a separate high-impact identity, consent, retention, and security review. |
| Automated SFW/predatory/IP screening and 24-hour dimming | Sentinel policy reference only. Any enforcement must be reviewable, auditable, appealable, and tested against false positives before activation. |
| Financial-growth targets and subscriber/revenue milestones | Business-planning claims, not application telemetry or user-facing stats. |
| Founder, company, and legal-entity fields | Confidential provenance metadata only. Public brand copy must use the approved Anom Originals identity and owner-approved attribution. |

## IP protection requirements

Keep the PDF private and checksum-verified. Do not expose confidential source documents, raw notebook exports, private identity material, internal revenue targets, or unreviewed schemas through public routes. Continue separating canonical source, supporting reference, prototype, review, and published route in the owner registry.

## Implementation conclusion

The PDF strengthens the current AO contract but does not authorize a public payment system, identity-passport workflow, or autonomous moderation enforcement. The next safe implementation remains the House/Guardian UI and a server-authoritative, non-cash GP/AC ledger with owner-controlled feature flags.
